About

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer with three years designing and operating distributed systems in on-prem datacenters and GCP. Day to day that means Kubernetes, Confluent Kafka, GitOps with ArgoCD, and infrastructure-as-code across both Linux and Windows environments.

My path here wasn't a straight line: I started in software as an intern at 18, spent several years teaching biology and building Python curriculum, then came back to engineering full-time. Years of teaching showed me that people rarely struggle for lack of ability; they struggle when no one gives them the context. And having spent years as an application developer myself, I know how frustrating it is when infrastructure feels like a black box. That's why I care about closing the gap between platform teams and application developers, and why I'm good at it.
